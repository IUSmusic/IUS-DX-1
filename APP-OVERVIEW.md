# IUS DX-1 Product Overview

**IUS DX-1** is a rhythm first drum production system for generating, analysing, arranging, editing and shaping drum material inside one focused workflow.

It is designed as a standalone application and VST3 plug-in workflow for producers who need more than static loops. The system supports five complete full kit source mixes, live groove capture, full track arrangement generation, timeline arrangement, MIDI editing, groove shaping, drum controls, effects, routing, soundbanks and LAB level runtime management.

## Main workflow

- **HOME:** generate, preview, regenerate and send five complete source mixes.
- **ENGINE:** capture MIDI or percussive audio and convert timing, density and feel into a Groove Profile.
- **GENERATE:** build longer structured drum arrangements from musical direction.
- **ARRANGE:** place source material and generated material on a timeline.
- **EDIT AND GROOVE:** edit MIDI, timing, velocity, swing, humanisation, density and groove lock.
- **DRUMS, FX AND ROUTING:** shape drum voices, effects, bus behaviour and signal flow.
- **LAB AND SETUP:** manage runtime status, soundbanks, verification, optional model routes and system setup.

## Product position

IUS DX-1 is not a generic loop pack and not a generic AI plug-in. Optional model routes may be available through LAB for users who want extended generation workflows, but the product is positioned as a complete drum production system built around source mix generation, groove capture, arrangement, editing and sound control.
